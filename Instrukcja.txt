### The "Starting Procedure"

**To start the car from 0 km/h, use one of these:**

* **Option A (The "Crest" Start):** `szczyt dnem, 200`
* **Option B (The "Wide Turn" Start):** `P 1 dnem, 150`
* **Option C (The "Gentle" Start):** `szczyt plus, 100`

---

### Master List of Working Commands

#### **Driving Straight (Maintaining Speed)**
* `100`
* `250 prosto`
* `500`

#### **Accelerating (Speeding Up)**
* `szczyt dnem, 200` (Full throttle)
* `szczyt +, 100` (Moderate gas)
* `L 1 _, 150` (Slight left curve, floor the gas)

#### **Braking (Slowing Down)**
* `hamuj, 100` (Standard brake)
* `pełne hamowanie, 50` (Emergency brake)
* `przyhamuj, 80` (Light tap)

#### **Turning (Steering)**
* `L 2, 100` (Wide left)
* `P 5, 40` (Sharp right - **Brake before this!**)
* `nawrót L, 20` (U-turn)

#### **Complex Chains (The "Rally" Way)**
* `szczyt dnem, 200, hamuj, P 3, 50, szczyt +, 100`
    * *Translation:* Floor it for 200m, brake, take a medium right for 50m, then accelerate moderately for 100m.

---

To ensure the car behaves and the code doesn't crash, follow these rules:
1.  **Numbers First:** Always write `100 prosto`, never `prosto 100`.
2.  **No Solo Modifiers:** Never start a command with `dnem`, `plus`, or `zduś`. Always put `szczyt` or a turn (`L 2`) before them.
3.  **Lowercase Only:** The parser expects `lewy`, not `LEWY`.
4.  **Commas Matter:** Use commas to separate different elements in a sequence.
